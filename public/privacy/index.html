Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

TAARV (also "we", "us") offers a set of online tools at taarv.com. This policy explains what information we handle, why, and what choices you have. We wrote it in plain words. It applies to everyone who uses the site, and we follow the laws of India, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

In short. Most tools work inside your browser, so the files you open there stay on your device. We only keep files if you sign in and choose to save them. We do not sell your information.

1. Files you open in a tool

When you open a photo, document, audio, video or other file in a tool, it is processed on your own device. It is not sent to our servers unless you press a save option while signed in.

2. Information we collect

  • Sign-in details. If you sign in with Google, we receive your account name, email address and a sign-in identifier from Google. We do not see your Google password.
  • Files you choose to save. If you save work or exports to "My saved files", we store those files and folder names so you can open them later.
  • Usage and plan records. If paid plans are switched on, we keep a count of free uses and your plan status so the limits work correctly.
  • Payment records. Payments are handled by our payment partner. We receive the result, amount and a reference. We never see or store your full card or bank details.
  • Messages to us. If you write to support@taarv.com, we keep your message and our reply.
  • Settings on your device. Tools keep your settings, recent work and auto-saved drafts in your browser storage on your device. You can clear this any time from your browser settings.

3. How we use it

  • To run the tools and the features you ask for, such as signing in and saving files.
  • To apply free-use limits and manage plans and payments.
  • To reply to your questions and fix problems.
  • To keep the service safe and to meet legal duties.

We use your information only for these purposes, based on your consent or on what is needed to give you the service.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run TAARV (for example sign-in, secure storage and payments), and they may use it only for that work. We may also share information when the law requires it, for example on a valid order from a court or government authority.

5. How long we keep it

Saved files stay until you delete them or close your account. Account and plan records stay while your account is active and for the period the law requires for payment and tax records. After that we delete or anonymise them.

6. Your choices and rights

You may ask us to show, correct, update or delete your personal information, to withdraw consent, or to close your account. You can delete saved files yourself in My saved files. Write to support@taarv.com from the email you signed in with.

7. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted connections and access controls. No online service is completely secure, so please keep your sign-in safe.

8. Cookies and browser storage

We use browser storage only for things you would expect: keeping you signed in, remembering settings and keeping drafts. We do not use it for advertising.

9. Families

Accounts are for people who are 13 or older, or the higher age that the law where you live sets for agreeing to the handling of personal data. The tools work without signing in, so anyone can use them without giving us any personal information. We do not track people or show ads to anyone. If an account was made for a young person without the agreement of a parent or guardian where the law needs it, write to support@taarv.com and we will help and remove it.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. For important changes we will tell you on the site.

11. Contact and grievances

For questions, requests or complaints about your information, write to support@taarv.com. We acknowledge within 2 working days and aim to resolve within 15 days.
